## 2. Program Components

### 1. Bridge Inside
Pre-release preparation, life skills, career exploration, and preliminary reentry planning where appropriate facility partnerships and permissions exist.

### 2. Bridge Reentry
Individualized Bridge Plans, mentorship, life-skills development, career preparation, resource navigation, and coordinated referrals.

### 3. Bridge Career Pathways
Career exploration and training pathways connected to employment, apprenticeship, vocational education, and postsecondary education.

### 4. Controls to Code
Signature industrial technology pathway connecting:
- Electrical fundamentals
- Instrumentation
- Sensors and transmitters
- 4–20 mA concepts
- PLC fundamentals
- Ladder logic
- Controls & industrial automation
- HMI/SCADA concepts
- Raspberry Pi & physical computing
- IoT & networking
- Python & software development
- APIs & databases
- Cloud concepts, edge computing & artificial intelligence

*Note: Controls and industrial automation remain central to the Controls to Code identity.*

### 5. Bridge Work
Employer partnerships, career preparation, work-based learning (where legally/financially appropriate), employment placement, apprenticeship connections, and retention support.

### 6. Future Construction & Trades Pathway (Planned / Inactive)
Potential future instruction and employer partnerships involving painting, construction, and skilled trades.  
*Current Status: NOT YET OPERATIONAL.*

## 3. Pilot Concept Specifications

- **Target Participant Count**: ~15 participants
- **Intensive Phase Duration**: ~12 weeks
- **Follow-up Duration**: Up to 12 months of continued support
- **Reassessment Policy**: Participant reassessment and additional pathway support when initial employment or training placement is not sustained.
